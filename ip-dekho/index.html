What Is My IP Address? Everything You Need to Know

What is my IP address? Your IP address is a unique number assigned by your Internet Service Provider that identifies your device on the internet. When you open IP Dekho, we instantly show your public IPv4 and IPv6 address, your city, region, country, ISP, timezone and hostname on a map. It helps you check your connection and location.

IPv4 vs IPv6

IPv4 is 32-bit address like 103.21.58.10. IPv6 is 128-bit like 2409:40c2:1002:: and is the newer version with more addresses. Our tool detects both. If your IPv6 shows not available, your ISP still uses IPv4 only.

Public IP vs Private IP - Why 192.168.1.1 has no location?

Private IP like 192.168.1.1 or 10.0.0.1 is only inside your home WiFi router. Every house has same private IP, so it has no global location. Public IP is visible on the internet and can be tracked to city-level location with ISP name. If you want to track a device, you need its public IP when it is online.

How does IP Lookup and Traceroute work?

When you enter an IP in IP Lookup, we query a database that maps IP ranges to ISP and city. Traceroute works by sending packets to target like 8.8.8.8 and listing each hop - your router, your ISP backbone, and destination server. This helps understand the internet route and approximate location.
