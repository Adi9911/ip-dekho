15 Common Spam SMS Examples in India

1. You've Won KBC Lottery!
Congratulations! You won Rs 25,00,000 in KBC lottery. Claim now: bit.ly/kbc25lakh
✅ Truth: Real KBC never sends bit.ly links. Check such lottery messages on ip-dekho Scam Checker.
2. SBI KYC Pending - Account Will Be Blocked
SBI Alert: Your KYC pending, account will be BLOCKED today. Update here: sbi-kyc-update.xyz
✅ Truth: Real SBI domain is only sbi.co.in. SBI never asks KYC via SMS. Verify any bank link with ip-dekho Link Checker.
3. Electricity Bill - Connection Will Be CUT Tonight
Dear Consumer, your electricity will be CUT at 9 PM. Pay immediately: bijli-bill-pay.online
✅ Truth: #1 scam in 2026. MSEDCL / BESCOM never cuts power at night via SMS link.
4. Verify Your Bank Account

5. India Post Package Delivery

6. Boss Needs Help - Gift Card Scam

11. Family Emergency

5 Ways to Identify Fake SMS in India [TRAI DLT Rule]

1. Sender ID: Real banks use SBIINB, HDFCBK, ICICIB. If it's a normal 10-digit mobile number, it's 100% fake.

2. Domain Name: Check domain in our Link Checker Tool. Real Flipkart is flipkart.com, not flipkart-offer.xyz

3. Urgency Words: BLOCKED, CUT TONIGHT, TODAY ONLY, LAST CHANCE = panic trick.

4. Short Links: bit.ly, tinyurl, cutt.ly - Real banks never use these.

5. Too Good To Be True: Free iPhone, 90% OFF, Lottery Winner.
